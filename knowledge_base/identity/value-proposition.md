# What I Bring to Your Organization

## The problem I solve

Most organizations collect more data than they can use. The gap between
raw data and reliable, decision-ready output is a data infrastructure
problem. I build and maintain that infrastructure.

I design the pipelines that move data from its source to where it needs
to go, clean, on time, and in a form that downstream teams can actually
use. This covers traditional data warehousing, large-scale lakehouse
architecture, and the data infrastructure that AI systems consume.

## What you get

A data engineer who takes full ownership of a data system from
requirements to production. I do not hand off half-built work. I have
built complete end-to-end systems independently: designed the
architecture, implemented the pipeline, handled failures, tested the
output, and documented the decisions.

My projects include:

- My latest data engineering project:is a full Medallion Architecture
  retail lakehouse on Databricks,data sources were OLTP postgresSQL database for sales data and Amazon S3 bucket for supply chain data, orchestrated with Apache Airflow on docker and
  transformed with dbt. It covers Bronze raw ingestion, Silver cleaning
  and validation, and Gold business-ready Data model, serving business users via 3-page Power bi report covers sales/supplies/workforce and Chatbot answers the ad-hoc questions.
  each layer: tested, documented, and independently reprocessable

- A Medallion Architecture lakehouse on Databricks processing
  e-commerce data through Bronze ingestion, Silver transformation,
  and Gold aggregation layers, with full data quality validation
  at each layer.

- A production RAG data pipeline powering an AI educational platform,
  indexing content for AI retrieval with grounded, citation-backed
  answers. This project received a perfect score of 200/200 as my
  graduation project.

- MoTaha AI, the assistant you are speaking to right now. I built
  it end to end: document ingestion, embedding, vector storage,
  hybrid retrieval using BM25 and dense search, and grounded LLM
  generation with a scope guard that refuses to answer when evidence
  is insufficient.
