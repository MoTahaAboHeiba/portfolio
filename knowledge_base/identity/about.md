# About Mohamed Taha

I am a Data Engineer and AI Engineer based in Egypt. I build the data

reliably from source to destination, lakehouses that store and transform
it at scale, and the data systems that AI applications consume.

I graduated with a Computer Science degree from the Faculty of Computers

I am currently targeting Data Engineer, Cloud Data Engineer, and Big
Data Engineer roles in Cairo, Egypt.

## What I build and why it matters

**Lakehouse architecture:** I build Medallion Architecture data lakehouses
on Databricks with Delta Lake. The business value is a single source of
truth for analytical and AI workloads, with full audit history, schema
enforcement, and the ability to reprocess historical data without
re-ingestion.

**ETL and data pipelines:** I design batch and incremental pipelines
orchestrated with Apache Airflow. The business value is reliable,
scheduled data movement with clear failure boundaries, retries, and
observable state.

**Data warehouse solutions:** I build dimensional models and star schemas
in SQL Server. The business value is a structured, queryable layer that
analysts and BI tools can consume without touching raw source systems.

**Data infrastructure for AI:** I build the ingestion, chunking,
embedding, and retrieval pipelines that feed AI systems. The business
value is AI applications that retrieve accurate, grounded information
instead of hallucinating from training data alone.

## How I work

I take full ownership of what I build. I design it, implement it, test
it, document the decisions, and hand off something that can be operated
and extended by someone else. I do not treat deployment as someone else's
problem.
