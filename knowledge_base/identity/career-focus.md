# Career Focus

## What I am available for

Full-time Data Engineer, Cloud Data Engineer, or Big Data Engineer roles
in Cairo, Egypt. Available immediately.

## What your organization gets

A data engineer who covers the full stack of data infrastructure work:

- Designing pipelines that are reliable, testable, and observable
- Building lakehouses that serve both analytical and AI workloads
- Implementing data quality checks that catch problems before they
  reach downstream consumers
- Building the data layer that AI systems consume: ingestion,
  embedding, vector storage, retrieval
- Documenting architecture decisions so the system can be operated
  and extended after handoff

## Why Data Engineering and AI Engineering together

The boundary between data engineering and AI engineering is collapsing.
Modern AI systems do not run on training data alone. They run on
retrieval pipelines, embedding infrastructure, and vector stores that
need the same reliability guarantees as any other production data system.
I build both sides of that boundary.

## What I am looking for

An organization that treats data infrastructure as a first-class
engineering discipline. I want to work on systems that process real
data at scale with real reliability requirements.

I am not a good fit for organizations that treat data engineering as
a reporting function or where pipeline failures are acceptable as long
as the dashboard eventually updates.
